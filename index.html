Unity Spark Project — Hello Puyallup Initiative Brief
Hello Puyallup

A Living Model for
Civic Belonging

Hello Puyallup is a mayor-led pilot that brings this vision to life — inviting neighbors to connect, listen, and build community together. Through storytelling, cultural exchange, and shared experiences, this initiative transforms neighborliness into a visible and powerful civic value.

Led by Unity Spark in partnership with the City of Puyallup, this pilot is intentionally designed from the outset to become a replicable national model for civic neighborliness.

Learn About the Pilot →
NOW
First
Hello Puyallup Public Art
Neighbor portraits go up throughout the community. Trained interviewers and photographers capture the faces and stories of Puyallup — displayed publicly for as long as possible so everyone can see who their neighbors really are.
NEXT
Then
Community Conversations
Informal gatherings at the Farmer's Market and outdoor pop-ups, running through October. Open to everyone — no registration required, just show up and say hello.

A citywide Folklorama-inspired celebration is being planned for a future year — designed to be annual, community-owned, and built on everything this pilot teaches us.
